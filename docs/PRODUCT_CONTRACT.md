# Good Company V0.1 Product Contract

## User

A founder, product leader, or operator using AI to turn ambiguous work into an accountable product or action.

## Desired progress

Move from an idea to an approved, testable direction without losing evidence, responsibility, uncertainty, or learning.

## Primary cognitive task

Understand what is actually being changed, distinguish evidence from assumptions, select the next valid decision, and retain human authority.

## Required evidence

Source-derived records, observations, assumptions, contradictions, and confidence must remain visibly distinct.

## Human approval points

The human owner approves the decision and any consequential external action. Colleagues may frame, retrieve, challenge, architect, coordinate, and implement only within the authority shown in the interface.

## Failure states

- An assumption is presented as fact.
- A recommendation appears automatically approved.
- A builder changes product intent.
- Missing evidence appears as negative evidence.
- The user cannot understand the current state or next action.
- An outcome is recorded but does not update future knowledge.

## Recovery path

The user can return to an earlier state, reclassify evidence, select Research or Reframe, decline approval, and record corrective learning.

## Success condition

A user can open a Room, frame the work, inspect evidence and assumptions, make and approve an explicit decision, and record an outcome that becomes Company Knowledge.
